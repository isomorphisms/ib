HTTP/1.0 returned 426; its existing HTTP/1.1 mode returned 200 with one redirect,

The private ingress previously accepted only ICU's older eight-field capture.

duplicate/unknown fields, non-absent ranges and unsupported transfer decoders,
and preserves the observed ISO-8859-1 charset as an observation. That charset
is not newly supported for decoding or navigation. Existing UTF-8 and
redirect-provenance admission requirements remain in force.

native test extends the established canonical appendfat → source-owner/PFD

not Android Binder; neither compilation nor this replay accepts C67.
