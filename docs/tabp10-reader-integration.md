`IB.Reader.Document` joins the maintained admission, HTML/Prepaint and
source-linked layout owners. The compiled projection receives every readback
observation explicitly. It does not acquire bytes, authenticate arbitrary
receipt-shaped text, or grant source admission to an Intent. The existing
native Activity remains the retained text reader until its trusted source and
worker lifecycle are actually wired.

The direct-DEX qualifier compiles the unchanged title, Intent and Activity
consumers and the integrated projection using the maintained managed-export
boundary while preserving the Activity contract. The independent observer has
fixed Unicode/link/wire expectations and distinguishes greedy from squared
raggedness at a declared code-point metric. It requires rejection of compiled
selector, admission and title mutants. Candidate DEX always comes from checked
Idriç; smali assembles only external observers.
